Research

Sustainable materials, waste valorization, artificial intelligence, and smart sensing.

Research interests

  • Sustainable construction materials
  • Waste valorization
  • AI for construction materials
  • Smart sensing: PZT and ERT

Low-carbon binders & carbon utilization

Low-carbon and clinker-free binders; alkali activation; CO₂ curing and mineral carbonation; carbon sequestration in cementitious materials.

Industrial by-products & waste valorization

Slag, fly ash, and other industrial by-products as supplementary cementitious materials and constituents of alternative binders.

AI for materials & infrastructure

Physics-informed neural networks, hydration heat prediction, pozzolanic reactivity prediction, and infrastructure condition monitoring.

Smart sensing & nondestructive evaluation

Multi-mode PZT sensing, electrical resistance tomography (ERT), and electrical impedance tomography (EIT) for cement-based materials.
