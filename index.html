Smart & Sustainable
Construction Materials Lab

Advancing low-carbon construction materials through materials science, sensing, and artificial intelligence.

Explore researchPublicationsMeet the team

Our research focuses on sustainable construction materials, waste valorization, carbon utilization, AI-enabled materials engineering, and smart sensing for cement-based materials and infrastructure.

Prospective students

We are recruiting graduate students for M.S., Ph.D., and integrated M.S.–Ph.D. programs.

Qualified students will receive tuition and monthly stipend support through the BK21 Program and externally funded research projects.

Interested applicants are welcome to contact me by email.
